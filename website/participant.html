What Are Your Bacteria Doing?

Bacteria can perform a range of functions, produce specific compounds, and be commonly associated with certain environments. Identifying their functional groups and common environments can help us to better understand how sink usage might influence the species that are able to survive and identify their potential source. These percentiles show how your sink compares to others for each category.
