How to Donate

All donations are processed securely through Duke University's giving portal.

  1. Visit gifts.duke.edu and search for PreMiEr (select PreMiEr: NSF Engineering Research Center).
  2. Enter your donation amount and click Next.
  3. Select One Time Gift, then continue to payment.
  4. After completion of payment, you will receive a confirmation email from Duke University for your tax records.
  5. Email us at sinkmicrobiome@duke.edu with the following information so we can ship your kit or swag:
    • Your first and last name
    • Your mailing address (US addresses only)
    • The donation option you selected (kit or swag tier)
    • For t-shirts: your preferred size and color
Important: The Duke giving portal does not automatically notify our team of your donation, and we are unable to see your personal information (such as your mailing address) on our end. You must send a follow-up email to sinkmicrobiome@duke.edu to receive your kit or swag.

Important Information

  • Tax deductibility. Duke University is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN: 56-0532129). Donations are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law. You will receive a donation receipt from Duke University for your records.
  • Use of funds. All donations support the Sink Microbiome Project directly, including sampling kit production and DNA sequencing costs.
  • One kit per household. Due to the scope of this project, each household may request no more than one sampling kit. For large-scale participation or institutional collaboration, please contact us at sinkmicrobiome@duke.edu.
  • US shipping only. Kits and swag are currently available for shipping to US addresses only.
  • Availability. Swag items are available while supplies last. If a requested item is out of stock, we will contact you by email to arrange a substitution.
  • One reward per donation. Each donation qualifies for one reward item at the corresponding tier level.
  • T-shirt sizing. Please specify your preferred size (S, M, L, or XL) and color (white or grey) in your follow-up email. If not specified, we will fulfill based on current availability.
  • No refunds. Donations to Duke University are non-refundable once processed.
  • Privacy. Your mailing address is collected solely for shipping purposes and will not be stored in our research database or shared with third parties. It will be handled in accordance with Duke University's privacy policy.

Want to give any amount without receiving swag?

We gratefully accept unrestricted donations of any size. Simply follow the same steps above and note "General Support" in your email — no physical reward will be sent.
