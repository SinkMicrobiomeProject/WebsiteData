Sink Microbiome Project

Hidden within every bathroom sink is a thriving microbial community — hundreds of bacterial species shaped by who uses the sink, what goes down the drain, and the personal care products in your routine. The Sink Microbiome Project is a nationwide citizen science initiative using full-length DNA sequencing to map the bacteria living in residential sinks across the United States. Enter your kit ID below to discover what's living in your sink, or explore the map and top taxa to see what we've found so far.
