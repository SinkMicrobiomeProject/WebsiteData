Sink Microbiome Project

Bathroom sinks are home to diverse communities of microbes that are influenced by how we use and clean them. The Sink Microbiome Project is a nationwide community science initiative that investigates how sink design, cleaning practices, and everyday activities shape these microbial communities. By participating, you are helping researchers better understand the microbes that live in our homes and identify ways to support healthier indoor environments.
